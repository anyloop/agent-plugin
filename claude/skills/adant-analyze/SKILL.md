# Adant Analyze

Before work, follow the [authentication preflight](../adant/references/authentication.md).

Follow the [durable project procedure](../adant/references/projects.md).
Select `agentKey: "adant-social-video"` with `adant_agent_start` after checking capabilities.

Use `projectType: "video_analysis"` and explicitly request analysis only. Include the
video URL or completed file id and the questions about hook, structure, pacing,
visuals, spoken content, promotion or product integration. AdAnt owns acquisition
and paid media understanding. Distinguish observed content from inference and
report acquisition failures; never claim a failed video was watched. Do not
launch broader research or generation unless requested.
