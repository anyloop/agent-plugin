# Initial Social Content Research

Before work, follow the [authentication preflight](../adant/references/authentication.md).

Follow the [durable project procedure](../adant/references/projects.md).

Use the [content research entrypoint](../content-research/SKILL.md) with
`projectType: "research"`. Run initial product and multi-platform social content research.
Pass the requested scope and reference URLs/report ids in the project brief.
The server owns data acquisition, private skills, analysis and report delivery.
Preserve citations and evidence gaps; do not substitute local/browser execution.

State the three criteria in the brief, so the report is built on them:

- **Outlier**: posts whose views and engagement run far above the author's
  follower count, or above a brand account's own usual reach. Raw view
  counts alone do not count.
- **Relevant**: about the product's category and its audience's pain. A viral
  post on another subject is dropped.
- **Fresh**: the last three months first and six at most, unless the user
  names another window.

Ask for clear, concise copy. Each finding, competitor description and lane
intro names an account or post and gives its measured number beside a
comparison, in one or two short sentences.

Before presenting the report, check it against those criteria. Name any lane
filled with posts older than six months, or with posts below the outlier bar,
instead of calling them outliers.
