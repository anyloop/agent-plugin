# Content Research

Before work, follow the [authentication preflight](../adant/references/authentication.md).

Follow the [durable project procedure](../adant/references/projects.md).
Select `agentKey: "adant-research-agent"` with `adant_agent_start` after checking capabilities.

Use `projectType: "research"`. Pass the product URL or supplied facts, audience,
platforms, scope, source reports, seed videos and freshness requirements in the
brief. Request a saved evidence-backed report or strategy brief. AdAnt owns
collection, analysis, private research skills and report creation. Preserve
returned citations, partial results and gaps; never claim unavailable evidence
was verified. Do not recreate the research workflow in the host conversation.
